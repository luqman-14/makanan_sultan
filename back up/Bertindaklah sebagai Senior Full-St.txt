* Framework: [PILIH: Next.js (React) / Vue (Nuxt) / HTML+Tailwind+JS]

5. Testimonial & Social Proof:																									
- Rating & Review pelanggan berfoto.																									
- Embed Feed Instagram / TikTok banner.																									
																									
6. Admin Dashboard Sederhana (Opsional/Tahap Lanjut):																									
- Toggle Status Stok Produk (Ready / Out of Stock).																									
- Rekap Pesanan Masuk & Status Pengiriman.
